# Notice

**Paper Crowns** is an unofficial solo digital adaptation of **Regicide**, a
cooperative card game designed by **Paul Abrahams** and published by
**[Badgers from Mars](https://www.badgersfrommars.com/regicide)**.

It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by them.

This implementation shares no text or art with the published game. Every string
was written for this app, and the cards are Unicode suit glyphs in web type.
It adapts the rules for solo play: computer-managed deck, automated power
resolution, on-screen forecasts, a tutorial, and a daily challenge with
shareable results.

Regicide is a great game and the physical copy is better than this.
[Buy one](https://badgersfrommars.com/en-us/collections/regicide) and support
the people who designed it.
